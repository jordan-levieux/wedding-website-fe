Theme

The Artistry of the Forest Floor

Our country's landscape is diverse and exciting. Mountains fall into deep green forests. Sunlight breaks through leaves and turns everything beneath them gold. Ferns, mosses and mushrooms gather quietly on the forest floor, while further south the landscape opens into fynbos, where some of the most extraordinary flowers in the world grow from seemingly impossible places. Travel a little further still and even the dry earth comes alive. In Namaqualand, an arid landscape can suddenly become a technicolour sea of flowers.

That sense of wonder is something we both love: the idea that beauty is rarely only one thing. It can be quiet-exuberant, delicate-hardy, wild-organised. Much like love, perhaps.

Our journey is bouldering at the gym, rainy bicycle rides, mountains on the other side of the world, far too many photographs, and eventually two engagement rings instead of one.

We have found that the best adventures are the ones you share.

Dress code

The Artistry of the Forest Floor

Semi-formal-extravagant

Think moss and mushrooms. Ferns unfurling between stones. Tiny flowers hidden amongst leaves. Bark, berries, lichen and seedpods. Shafts of light falling through trees. The deep greens of the forest, the burgundy of autumn leaves, the warm ochres of earth and fungi, and the unexpected flashes of blue, orange, yellow and pink found in South Africa's extraordinary flora.

You'll see some of those colours woven through the photographs and botanical illustrations across this website. Consider them inspiration rather than instruction.

We would love our guests to add to the landscape.

Our dress code is semi-formal-extravagant. Be the startingly beautiful gem in the undergrowth. Rich jewel tones, earthy shades, soft botanical colours and bright wildflower hues all belong here. There is no need to disappear into beige, black or muted wedding neutrals, unless that is what makes you happiest, with the proviso that I have not found a neon light in the bushes despite many years of searching.

Wear something beautiful. Wear something joyful. Wear a colour you love.

Imagine all of us together beneath the trees, like one wonderfully dressed, slightly unruly forest floor.
