Our story

Somewhere between climbing walls, mountain paths, long conversations and a friendship neither of us was quite willing to risk, we found each other.

The outdoors has always been where we feel most ourselves. In forests and on mountains, on bicycles in the pouring rain, on trails far from home, and in all the wild little places that make you stop for a moment and look around.

So it feels only right that this next adventure begins surrounded by the people we love, in a place that feels a little wild too. We would love for you to join us as we celebrate the beginning of our life together, beneath the trees and under the South African sky.
